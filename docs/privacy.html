Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 13, 2025

1. Introduction

CPFT ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Cross-Platform File Transfer application ("Application").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Device Name: Custom device names you set for identification
  • Feedback: Information you provide when contacting support
  • App Usage Data: How you interact with the Application

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Platform (Android/iOS/Web), OS version, app version
  • Network Information: Local network usage for device discovery
  • Performance Data: App crashes, errors, and performance metrics
  • Usage Analytics: Feature usage patterns (with your consent)

2.3 Files You Transfer

  • No Storage: We do not store, access, or transmit your files
  • Local Only: All file transfers occur directly between devices on your local network
  • No Monitoring: We do not monitor, scan, or analyze transferred content

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Service Provision

  • Enable device discovery and connection
  • Facilitate peer-to-peer file transfers
  • Provide customer support and troubleshooting

3.2 Application Improvement

  • Analyze app performance and identify issues
  • Improve user experience and add features
  • Debug technical problems

3.3 Communication

  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Send important service updates
  • Provide information about new features

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except as described in this policy.

4.2 Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in:

  • App analytics and performance monitoring
  • Error reporting and debugging
  • Customer support services

These providers are contractually obligated to maintain confidentiality and security.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to:

  • Legal process (subpoena, court order)
  • Law enforcement requests
  • Protection of rights, property, or safety

4.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new entity.

5. Data Security

5.1 Local Network Security

  • All file transfers occur on your local network only
  • No data transmission over the internet
  • Device discovery uses standard network protocols

5.2 Application Security

  • Industry-standard security measures
  • Regular security updates
  • Secure communication protocols

5.3 Your Responsibilities

  • Keep your device and passwords secure
  • Use strong network security practices
  • Encrypt sensitive files before transfer

6. Data Retention

6.1 Personal Information

Data Type Retention Period
Device names and settings While you use the app
Support communications For customer service purposes
Analytics data Aggregated and anonymized

6.2 Automatic Deletion

  • No personal data is permanently stored on our servers
  • Local data is managed by your device
  • You can delete app data through device settings

7.1 Access and Control

  • Access: Request information about data we have about you
  • Correction: Update or correct your information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data in a portable format

7.2 Opt-Out Options

  • Notifications: Control notification preferences
  • Device Permissions: Manage location and storage permissions

7.3 Account Deletion

You can delete your account and associated data by:

  • Uninstalling the application
  • Contacting support for complete data removal
